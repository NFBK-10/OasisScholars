Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

OASISSCHOLARS respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using our scholarship website, owner portal, and related services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, messages, owner account information, scholarship posts, uploaded scholarship images, uploaded documents, application links, and other content submitted through the website.

Our hosting and service providers may also process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, page requests, authentication logs, and security events.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the website, manage owner accounts, publish scholarship opportunities, display scholarship details, respond to messages, protect the website, prevent misuse, improve our services, and comply with legal or platform requirements.

Public Scholarship Content

Scholarship posts submitted by owners may be displayed publicly. This can include the scholarship title, provider, summary, deadline, award information, application link, full details, uploaded images, and uploaded documents. Do not upload private, confidential, or copyrighted content unless you have permission to publish it.

Owner Accounts

Owner accounts are used to create and manage scholarship opportunities. Account information may include name, email address, authentication records, and posts connected to that account. Owners are responsible for keeping login details secure.

Cookies and Browser Storage

The website may use browser storage, cookies, or authentication tokens to keep owners logged in, support dashboard features, and improve security. Clearing browser storage may require you to log in again.

Third-Party Services

GitHub Pages hosts the static website. Supabase provides authentication, database, and storage services. These providers may process technical, account, and uploaded information according to their own policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, when required by law, to protect rights and safety, to investigate abuse, or as part of an organizational transfer.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS, access controls, Row Level Security rules, upload limits, and content security policies. No website can guarantee complete security. Use strong passwords and avoid sharing login credentials.

Data Retention

We keep information as long as needed to operate the website, provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, protect against abuse, or comply with legal obligations. Public scholarship posts may remain available until removed by an authorized owner or administrator.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable. Owners may remove their own scholarship posts from the dashboard when their account has permission.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

International Visitors

The website may be accessed from different countries. By using the site, you understand that information may be processed by service providers in locations outside your country.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact OASISSCHOLARS through the contact information listed on the Contact page.
